## 🏛️ Flagship Architectures & Open Source Systems

### 1. [🧠 CogniSpace — Modern Collaborative Workspace & AI Engine](https://github.com/karthik1122-code/cognispace)
> **Full-Stack Notion-grade workspace with headless block editor, collapsible toggles, Kanban sprint database, and real-time Gemini AI streaming.**

- **Headless TipTap Architecture:** Custom ProseMirror node schemas for Notion-style `ToggleBlock` (`<details>/<summary>`), interactive nested task items, and floating bubble formatting menus.
- **Optimistic Concurrency Control (OCC):** Autosave pipeline debounced at 800ms with version tracking to eliminate write-race hazards without blocking typing flow.
- **Sub-18ms SSE AI Stream:** Server-Sent Events endpoint streaming Google Gemini tokens directly into the document canvas with zero-latency browser `AbortSignal` cancellation.
- **Resilient Multi-Layer Data Tier:** Express 5 backend with seamless fallback between MongoDB Atlas and embedded in-memory database engines.
- 🔗 **[Explore Live Demo](https://cognispace-sigma.vercel.app) · [View Architecture & Code](https://github.com/karthik1122-code/cognispace)**

---

### 2. [⚡ RecoverFlow — AI-Powered Revenue Recovery Engine](https://github.com/karthik1122-code/recoverflow)
> **Policy-first AI revenue recovery and dispute mitigation engine for Razorpay AI Buildathon.**

- **Deterministic Policy Layer:** Classifies failed payment causes (insufficient funds, network timeout, mandate failure) and bounds retry actions through strict financial guardrails.
- **Cryptographic Security:** Verifies `X-Razorpay-Signature` HMACs on incoming webhooks to ensure zero unauthenticated interventions.
- **Audit-First Queueing:** Provides immutable decision logs and exception queues for human-in-the-loop validation of low-confidence scenarios.
- 🔗 **[View Repository](https://github.com/karthik1122-code/recoverflow)**

---

### 3. [☁️ Nimbus (Insight AI) — Enterprise SaaS Platform](https://github.com/karthik1122-code/nimbus)
> **Modern AI Analytics SaaS platform with interactive 3D telemetry and high-conversion product showcases.**

- **Dynamic Telemetry Visualizations:** Animated Data Globe node tracking, live insight metrics feed, and tabbed inspection engine.
